Artefacts

Modules 1–5 – Foundation in Security and Awareness for the Digital Age

Researched Industry 4.0 and security risk – what IoT and AI can do to operations, how new threats arise. Raise awareness and analysis of cyber-physical resilience incidents.

Section 6 – Assessing the Risks and Working with Your Team

Prepared a Risk Analysis Report on “Pampered Pets”. Used NIST Cybersecurity Framework along with qualitative risk matrix. Engaged in peer evaluation of communication and coordination improvement.

Parts 7–10 – Vulnerability Assessment Models

The limitations of CVSS were analyzed, and SSVC and EPSS were evaluated for the prioritisation (contextualised, data-driven) vulnerability. Casettari: Data wrangling in data center security.

Unit 11 – ICS Risk Assessment and DR Plan

Created a risk model (Monte Carlo simulation and FMEA) to evaluate supply chain and quality risks quantitatively. End-to-End Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity Plan in place.

Final Piece of Work Assessment – Unit 11 vs Unit 6

This section compares the Unit 6 Risk Identification to the Unit 11 Final Project, to demonstrate scope has matured, risk maturity has increased and what lessons have been learned.

Aspect Unit 6 Status Unit 11 Outcome Commentary
Scope On-location activities and NIST QRA. Quantitative modelling in a worldwide digital supply chain (Monte Carlo + FMEA). Expanded from a local to an enterprise.
Risks Operational dependency and manual workflows. Failure of automation and dependency on a supplier (~10% risk for shortages per year). Risk transferred from local to systemic cyber physical problems.
Controls POS upgrades and basic backups. Replication in the cloud, predictive analytics and MFA. Controls transformed into more complex business resilience.
Continuity Manual backup and recovery processes. like formal DR/BCP with ~1-minute RPO and be able for fail-over. From natural time-like continuity to engineered space-like stability with distances.
Lessons Learned Requires ongoing risk documentation and role clarity. A redundancy design, that includes qualitative and quantitative methods. Unified risk approach from tech to people/process.
