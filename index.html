Overview

How to read this portfolio

This site presents my assessed portfolio for the module. It is structured to match the marking criteria and includes at least one artefact from each unit, clear evidence of my individual contributions, a comparison of Unit 11 final project vs Unit 6 status, and a reflective piece capped at 1,000 words.

  • Academic integrity: Harvard referencing throughout.

Reflective Piece

≤ 1,000 words

The reflective piece must be no more than 1,000 words. Suggested structure using Rolfe (2001):

  1. What: briefly describe the incident/activity/outcome.
  2. So what: analyse your emotions/behaviours, relate to literature and frameworks (e.g., CVSS, SSVC, EPSS), evaluate implications.
  3. Now what: concrete actions and how learning will inform future practice.

If using video, include English subtitles and upload the transcript file alongside submission.

References

Harvard style

Use Harvard referencing. Every in‑text citation must appear in the reference list and vice versa.

  • Rolfe, G., Freshwater, D. and Jasper, M. (2001) Critical reflection in nursing and the helping professions: a user’s guide. Basingstoke: Palgrave Macmillan.
  • University of Edinburgh (n.d.) Reflection Toolkit. Available at: https://www.ed.ac.uk/ (Accessed: Day Month Year).
