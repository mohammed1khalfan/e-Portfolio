Overview

This e-Portfolio presents my assessed work for the Security & Risk Management module. It includes artefacts from each unit, my individual contributions, a comparative evaluation between Unit 6 and Unit 11, and a 1 000-word reflective piece.

  • Harvard referencing throughout.
  • Clear structure and accessible format.
  • Evidence-based presentation of learning outcomes.

Artefacts

Units 1–5 – Foundation of Security and Digital Risk Awareness

Explored Industry 4.0 and security risks, understanding how IoT and AI reshape operations while introducing new threat vectors. Developed awareness of cyber-physical resilience and incident analysis.

Unit 6 – Risk Identification and Team Collaboration

Produced a Risk Identification Report for “Pampered Pets”. Applied the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and qualitative risk matrix. Engaged in peer evaluation to improve communication and coordination.

Units 7–10 – Vulnerability Assessment Frameworks

Critically analysed CVSS limitations and evaluated SSVC and EPSS for context-aware and data-driven vulnerability prioritisation. Applied concepts to data centre security scenarios.

Unit 11 – Final Project: Quantitative Risk Assessment and DR Plan

Built a quantitative risk model using Monte Carlo simulation and FMEA to assess supply-chain and quality risks. Developed a comprehensive Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity Plan.

Contributions

  • Risk Modelling Lead (Unit 11): Developed Monte Carlo and FMEA framework for risk quantification and proposed an active-active DR design.
  • Risk Identification (Unit 6): Drafted QRA matrix and mapped controls to NIST CSF categories to establish baseline controls.
  • Framework Analysis (Units 7–10): Proposed hybrid SSVC + EPSS model for vulnerability prioritisation within data centre operations.
